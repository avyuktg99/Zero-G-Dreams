Project 01 · in progress

Sleep, but for astronauts.

Coming
soon

A lunar day is 29.5 Earth days long. The work still happens at 3 a.m.

Crews lose sleep first and notice it last, so fatigue quietly becomes the thing that breaks a mission. We're building a planner that takes a mission timeline, finds the safest sleep windows, and tells a crew when they're running on empty — before they find out the hard way.

It runs on a plain laptop, offline. Fancy cloud dashboards are useless when the nearest server is 384,000 km away.

  • ✓ The model — nine steps, 20 checks against published NASA data
  • ◐ The app — works, still growing new screens
  • ○ Real flight data — needs a launch we don't have
  • 66%less fatigue in our 28-sol simulation
  • 8.5hwhat NASA says a crew needs
  • 6.1hwhat ISS crews actually get
